The free academic edition and its limits

Tellus 2D runs as the free academic edition when no licence key is entered, so students and teachers can use it without a key, a registration or an internet connection. It is the full program with a few limits on model size, soil models and structures, which are large enough for course exercises and small enough to keep it out of design work. This page lists every limit, explains what happens when a model goes beyond one, and shows how to fit a teaching model inside them.

Who it is for

The free academic edition is for students, teachers and researchers at universities, colleges and schools, and for anyone learning the finite element method on their own. It covers teaching, coursework, student exercises and projects, theses, non-commercial research and self-study, wherever in the world you are.

It is not for design, consulting, checking or any other paid work for a client or an employer, and its results must not appear in design reports, tender documents or other commercial deliverables. Companies and paid projects need a licence key, and so does commercial work done by a student or a university.

Every start opens on the choice between entering a licence key and clicking Continue with the free academic edition, so a key can be entered at any start. In the free edition the top bar shows Free academic edition, which you can click to see the limits or to enter a key.

How each limit is counted

  • Mesh. The count is the number of 15-node triangles in the generated mesh, as reported in the log after Generate mesh. Tellus meshes the model again before it calculates, so the limit applies to the mesh that would actually be solved.
  • Phases. Every phase after the initial phase counts, whatever its type, so a Safety (strength reduction) phase or a Consolidation phase uses one of the two.
  • Soil models. The check looks at every soil material in the project, so a Hardening Soil material that is defined but not used still stops the calculation. Delete it or change its model.
  • Structures. Plates, geogrids and struts or anchors are counted separately, so a model may have two of each at the same time.

3

What is not limited

Everything outside the table above works as in the licensed program. This includes the soil layers, boreholes and drawn soil clusters, the number of materials with the three free soil models, interfaces on plates, loads, water levels and flow conditions, and the Initial phase by the K0 procedure or by Gravity loading. Plastic, Consolidation and Safety phases are all available within the two-phase limit.

The free edition also keeps the built-in chat assistant, the MCP server for Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Codex, the command-line options, saving and opening .tellus projects and the PDF report. It needs no internet connection, and it has no time limit, so a student can keep it for a whole degree.

When a model is beyond a limit

A model that is beyond a limit is still built, edited and saved as usual, and only the calculation is refused. Calculate then names the limit that stops it and the size of the model, for example:

The mesh has 1240 elements; the Free academic edition calculates up to 1000.

write to tellusfem@gmail.com.

Tellus checks the soil models first, then the phases, the plates, the geogrids, the struts and anchors, the piles and finally the mesh, so it reports one limit at a time. After you fix it, Calculate may report the next one. The chat assistant and an assistant connected through MCP get the same message, and they are told to report the limit to you rather than try to work around it.

A project saved in the free edition opens and calculates unchanged once a licence key is entered, so students who later get a key keep their work.

5

Watermark on reports and plots

Every page of a PDF report from the free edition carries a light diagonal watermark, "Tellus Free Academic Edition - not for commercial use", and a line at the foot of the page that says the report is for teaching and learning only, not for design or commercial work. The plots in Output carry the same watermark text in a corner.

The watermark is part of the academic conditions, so it must not be removed, cropped or hidden. A figure from the free edition can go into a thesis, a paper or a presentation with the watermark visible and a citation of Tellus.

6

Fitting a model in the limits

Most teaching problems fit in the free edition, because the default model has about 420 elements and a 2 m strip footing on sand over clay calculates with room to spare. When a model is too large, these changes usually bring it within the limits.

  • Use symmetry. Model half of a symmetric footing, embankment or excavation, with a roller boundary on the axis, which halves the mesh.
  • Coarsen the mesh. Make the element size factor larger and generate the mesh again, then read the new element count in the log.
  • Trim the boundaries. Keep the model wide and deep enough that the boundaries do not change the result, but no larger, because distant soil adds elements without changing the answer.
  • Merge phases. Combine construction steps that a lesson does not need to separate, so that a typical exercise of initial stresses, one construction phase and one Safety phase uses exactly the two phases allowed.
  • Use the free soil models. Mohr-Coulomb teaches failure and the factor of safety, and Soft Soil teaches the compression of soft clay, which covers most course work.

A coarser mesh changes the result, so check that the settlement or factor of safety barely changes between two mesh sizes before you rely on it. This mesh study is a good exercise in its own right.

7

Licence keys

A licence key lifts every limit and removes the watermark. Keys are issued free to university research groups whose work goes beyond the free edition, as trial keys to companies that want to assess Tellus, and as commercial licences for design and consulting.

To ask for one, use the contact form or write to tellusfem@gmail.com. For a research key, write from your university address and name your supervisor or course, and for a trial or commercial key, name your company and the projects you have in mind. Enter the key by clicking Free academic edition in the top bar, and activation then needs the internet once.
